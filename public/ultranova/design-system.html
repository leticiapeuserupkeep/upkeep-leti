Design System

One typeface, one brand purple, and one size rhythm. Controls are 32, 40, and 48. Tags and badges are 20, 24, and 32. Avatars follow the controls, plus 72 for the chat.

Color

One row per color, light to dark. The token is --un- plus the row and the step, for example --un-brand-500. A name means a product token already points there. “Later” is an open step. Success and “done” use the ink step, never a separate green.

Motion

Soft and short. Hover lifts 2px. Entrances rise and unblur in 0.7s. Menus and toasts fade in 0.2–0.3s. The assistant blinks and waves. prefers-reduced-motion cuts transitions to a frame and stops loops.

Icons

One stroke icon, 1.5px, round caps, on a 24 view box. 16 in menus, 20 in the UI, 24 when the control is large. Click a tile to copy its name.

Buttons

Primary is ink. Secondary is a soft grey. Heights match every other control: 32, 40, 48.

Fields

Same 32, 40, and 48 as the buttons. Focus draws a brand ring. Errors use red and say what to do next.
